Privacy Policy

Moja Biblioteka

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Moja Biblioteka ("the App") accesses, uses, stores, and shares information.

The App is designed primarily as a local-first personal library application. Most information entered by the user is stored directly on the user's device.

1. Developer

Moja Biblioteka is developed and maintained by Kamil Pietka.

For privacy-related questions, users may contact the developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.

2. Information Stored on the Device

The App may store information related to the user's personal library, including:

  • book titles and authors,
  • ISBN numbers,
  • reading status,
  • personal notes,
  • lending history,
  • book-related metadata entered or selected by the user.

This information is stored locally on the user's device unless a feature explicitly requires communication with an external service.

The developer does not have access to locally stored library data unless the user voluntarily provides that information, for example when contacting support.

3. Camera Permission

The App may request permission to access the device camera in order to scan ISBN barcodes or QR codes.

Camera access is used only when the user activates a scanning feature. The App does not intentionally store, upload, sell, or share camera images for advertising or tracking purposes.

Users may deny or revoke camera permission through their device settings. Core library functionality that does not require scanning may continue to work without camera access.

4. Book Metadata and External Services

The App may communicate with third-party book-information services, such as Open Library, to retrieve information about books.

When this functionality is used, the App may send information such as an ISBN number or book search query to the external service.

External services may also receive standard technical information associated with internet requests, such as the user's IP address, according to their own privacy policies and technical practices.

5. Personal and Sensitive User Data

The App does not intentionally collect or sell sensitive personal data such as:

  • financial information,
  • precise location,
  • contacts,
  • SMS or call history,
  • health information,
  • government identification information.

The App does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell user data.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services only when necessary to provide functionality requested by the user, such as retrieving book metadata from an external book database.

The App does not intentionally share locally stored personal library information with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The App does not intentionally use user information for behavioral advertising or cross-app tracking.

If analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or similar third-party services are introduced in a future version of the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Data Security

The developer takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App.

Network communication is intended to use secure HTTPS connections where supported by the external service.

Because most application data is stored locally, users are also responsible for securing access to their device and maintaining appropriate device security settings.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by the App remains on the user's device until the user removes it.

Depending on the device and operating system, local application data may be deleted by:

  • removing individual records from the App,
  • clearing the App's stored data,
  • uninstalling the App.

If future versions of Moja Biblioteka introduce user accounts, cloud synchronization, or server-side storage, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain account deletion, server-side retention, and deletion procedures.

10. Children's Privacy

Moja Biblioteka is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, third-party services, or data practices change.

The current version of the Privacy Policy will remain available at:

https://mojabiblioteka.kamilpietka.dev/privacy/

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's handling of user information, please use the developer contact details provided on the Google Play Store listing for Moja Biblioteka.
